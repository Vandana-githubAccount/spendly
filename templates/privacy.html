Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your account details (name, email, password) and the expense data you log, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide core Spendly features — logging expenses, generating summaries and category breakdowns, and keeping your account secure. We do not use your financial data for advertising or sell it to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in Spendly's database and retained for as long as your account remains active. We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting or email delivery services) to operate the app. These providers only process data as needed to deliver their service and are not permitted to use it for other purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on the Spendly website.
